## Saving your work 

## What this exercise taught me
This exercise taught me how to use Stash in git.  
To make a save of current work without committing, I use `git stash`.  
Said command makes a stack of the current working directory, and I am to fix the bug and commit it

Finally, I use `stash restore` to restore my old changes, and then add the new line at the end, essentially fixing the bug.
